Paywall Bot System Map

How @Yaytseslavvip_bot mutes new members of the VIP group, takes their payment, unmutes them, and mutes them again when their time runs out. Everything runs in one Cloudflare Worker with one database.

What happens when someone pays

  1. Someone joins the VIP group. Telegram tells the Worker at /telegram/owner, which checks Telegram's secret token so nobody else can pose as Telegram.
  2. The bot mutes them (they can read but not post) and sends them a payment menu. They can also open the bot and send /start.
  3. The Worker creates a checkout with Paystack or NOWPayments, saves a pending payment in D1, and sends the buyer the payment link.
  4. The buyer pays. The provider calls /webhooks/… with a signed message. The Worker checks the signature, then asks the provider directly to confirm the amount.
  5. The payment is marked paid, the member's access date is saved, the bot unmutes them in the group and sends a confirmation.
  6. When a monthly plan runs out, the every-minute job mutes them again and sends a Renew button.

Safety built in

  • A payment counts only after the signature checks out and the provider confirms it. A forged webhook is ignored.
  • Each payment is processed once, even if the provider sends it twice.
  • Partial crypto payments don't unlock access.
  • Keys live in Cloudflare secrets. Creator keys are encrypted in the database.
  • Request tracing is off, because Telegram puts the bot token in its URLs.
